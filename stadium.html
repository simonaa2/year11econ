This simulation covers NESA Economics & Business syllabus concepts:

Supply & Demand

Venues supply seats; fans demand tickets. The chart shows where the market clears.

Consumer Surplus

The gap between what you'd pay (WTP) and what you actually pay. Higher = better deal for the fan.

Producer Surplus

Revenue the venue earns above its costs. Venue managers are competing for this.

Deadweight Loss

When a fan is priced out — willing to buy but can't afford it. The transaction that "should" happen doesn't.

Pricing Strategies

Cost-plus, skimming, penetration, dynamic — each trades off fill rate vs revenue per seat.

Non-Price Determinants

Market shocks shift demand curves — income, tastes, prices of substitutes, expectations.
