🔬 Research Model & Mathematical Synergy Formula:

The Multifactor Team Dynamics Index evaluates how domestic club familiarity and shared positional tactical systems compound national team performance:

S = (T_i × 0.25 + C_d × 0.40 + A_t × 0.20 + Q_c × 0.15), normalised [40 – 95]
  • T_i (Tactical Integration): Alignment of club tactical roles and league tactical familiarity.
  • C_d (Club Density): Measure of co-playing frequency in shared domestic clubs (e.g. Barcelona/Real Madrid for Spain, Bayern for Germany).
  • A_t (Age Alignment): Peak athletic age synergy across squad core.
  • Q_c (Quality Concentration): World-class player depth distribution.
