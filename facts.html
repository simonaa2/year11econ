Economics Concepts — Spot Them in the Data
PRICE ELASTICITY
Final tickets are inelastic — demand barely drops even at $5,000. Group stage is more elastic — fans choose based on price.
CONSUMER SURPLUS
A fan willing to pay $2,000 who gets a ticket for $800 earns $1,200 in consumer surplus. The ballot system creates enormous surplus for winners.
PRICE DISCRIMINATION
Cat 1–4 tickets for the same match, 4 different prices. Airlines: economy vs business for the same flight. Classic 3rd degree.
DEADWEIGHT LOSS
1.3M+ applicants couldn't get tickets. Willing buyers, no transaction = lost economic welfare.
YIELD MANAGEMENT
Airlines adjusting fares in real-time — +68% surge during the tournament as seats fill. Book early or pay more.
MARKET FAILURE
Cat 4 local tickets at $29 = govt intervention to ensure equity and access. Without it, all seats would clear at much higher prices.
