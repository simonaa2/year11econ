Model Answer Structure

A strong extended response follows this structure. Each paragraph has a job.

Introduction
What to do:
  • Define key terms from the question (e.g. financial markets, interest rates, labour market)
  • State your argument — what will your response show?
  • Briefly outline what each paragraph will cover
📝 "Financial markets play a central role in allocating resources within the Australian economy. The Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) uses the cash rate as its primary monetary policy tool to influence interest rates across the economy. This response will examine how recent changes to the cash rate have affected business spending and employment outcomes, drawing on current data and media commentary."
Body ¶1 — Financial Markets & RBA
What to do:
  • Point: State the RBA's current monetary policy stance with data
  • Evidence: Current cash rate, CPI figure, date of last change
  • Explain: Why did the RBA take this action? (inflation target, economic conditions)
  • Link: How does this affect business borrowing costs?
📝 "The RBA [cut/held/raised] the cash rate to X% in [month], citing [key reason from board minutes]. With inflation [above/within/below] the 2–3% target band at X%, the RBA's decision aimed to [stimulate/cool] economic activity. This directly affects the cost of borrowing for businesses across Australia's debt and equity markets..."
Body ¶2 — Impact on Business Spending
What to do:
  • Point: Explain the transmission mechanism to business investment
  • Evidence: Data / quote from one of your articles
  • Explain: Analyse how lower/higher rates affect business confidence and spending
  • Link: Connect to labour demand and job creation
📝 "Lower interest rates reduce the cost of capital for firms, incentivising business investment. According to [Article 1 source], '[direct quote or stat]'. This illustrates that changes in monetary policy directly influence business confidence and investment decisions, which in turn affects the demand for labour..."
Body ¶3 — Labour Market Outcomes
What to do:
  • Point: Link financial market conditions to labour market data
  • Evidence: Current unemployment, underemployment, WPI figures (ABS)
  • Explain: How do current labour market trends reflect monetary policy?
  • Link: Reference Article 2 and Fair Work Commission / institutions
📝 "The impact of monetary policy on business spending is reflected in current labour market data. The ABS reported an unemployment rate of X% in [period], while the Wage Price Index grew by X% annually. [Article 2 source] notes that '[quote]', demonstrating that [link to course content]..."
Conclusion
What to do:
  • Restate your argument — what has your response shown?
  • Synthesise: how do financial markets and labour markets connect?
  • Evaluate: is the current policy approach effective?
📝 "In conclusion, the RBA's recent monetary policy decisions have had significant implications for both financial markets and labour market outcomes in Australia. The transmission mechanism from cash rate changes through to business investment and employment demonstrates the interconnected nature of these markets. While current data suggests [evaluation], the effectiveness of monetary policy ultimately depends on [nuanced point]..."
