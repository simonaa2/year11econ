Yield Management
Sell the same seat at different prices to different passengers based on WTP — the core of airline pricing strategy.
💰
Consumer Surplus
What you were willing to pay minus what you actually paid. Higher surplus = better deal for the passenger.
🏆
Price Competition
Price too high → empty seats, lost revenue. Price too low → full plane but small profit. Find the sweet spot.
⚖️
Deadweight Loss
When a passenger wants a seat but the price is above their WTP — a mutually beneficial trade that doesn't happen.
