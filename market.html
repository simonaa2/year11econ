📚 Microeconomics Concepts

Price Elasticity: If PED < -1, demand is elastic — a 1% price rise causes >1% drop in quantity demanded. Leisure travellers are elastic; business travellers are inelastic.

Oligopoly: You can see competitor prices. This is a strategic game — if you all price high, passengers pay more. If you undercut, you win share but start a price war.

Producer Surplus: Your profit per seat = price − average cost. Total producer surplus = shaded area on the supply/demand chart.

📚 Your Role in the Market

Consumer Surplus: The difference between what you were willing to pay and what you actually paid. A cheaper flight = higher surplus = more points!

Law of Demand: The lower the price, the more likely you (and others like you) are to book. You demonstrate this law by your own choices.

Market Power: When many airlines compete, you benefit. When only one airline exists (monopoly), they can charge much more — watch what happens if the teacher triggers a Merger shock!
